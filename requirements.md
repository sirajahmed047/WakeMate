# WakeMate Landing Page - Requirements Document

## Project Overview

**Product Name:** WakeMate  
**Platform:** iOS App (Built with Expo)  
**Core Feature:** AI-powered wake-up calls that engage users in conversation to ensure they're fully awake

## Product Description

WakeMate is an unconventional alarm app that replaces traditional alarm notifications with intelligent phone calls. When your alarm triggers, an AI assistant calls you directly and engages you in a conversation, asking questions to verify you're awake and alert.

## Technical Stack

- **Mobile Framework:** Expo (React Native)
- **AI Voice Calling:** VAPI
- **Phone Infrastructure:** Twilio
- **Database:** Firestore
- **Scheduling:** Custom scheduler for call triggers

## Landing Page Objectives

### Primary Goals
1. Clearly communicate WakeMate's unique value proposition
2. Showcase how the AI calling feature works
3. Drive iOS app downloads

- People who struggle with traditional alarms
- Heavy sleepers who need extra motivation to wake up

- Anyone seeking a more engaging wake-up experience
